# Homepage / Landing Page

Inspired by:

- SyncSpace Official Signature Design

Characteristics:

- Top Pill Tag: `✨ The Workspace for Teams` with subtle purple border and light fill.
- Hero Headline: Large, bold typography with Electric Indigo gradient accent on key phrase (`next generation`).
- Dual CTA: Primary solid Electric Indigo button (`bg-[#4648d4] text-white border-t border-white/20`) + Secondary outline button (`Book a demo`).
- Product Showcase Mockup: Elevated dark-mode dashboard window container with macOS control dots, purple aura glow background shadow, metric progress bars, mini Gantt chart, and dark Kanban columns.
- Trust Bar: Monochromatic company logo strip ("Acme Corp", "Vercel", "Supabase", "Linear", "Raycast").
- Feature Grid: 4 cards ("Real-time collaboration", "Project Planning", "Task Management", "Activity tracking") with an embedded SVG productivity area chart.
- Kanban Deep-Dive: Split layout with bullet list, checkmarks, and Kanban card preview.
- Pricing Section: 3 cards (Starter $0, Pro $12 Featured with glowing Electric Indigo border & "MOST POPULAR" pill, Enterprise Custom) + Monthly/Yearly toggle with 20% discount badge.
- Dark CTA Banner: Centered "Ready to build better?" section on dark background (`bg-[#09090b]`).
- Footer: Multi-column links (PRODUCT, PLATFORM, COMPANY) + bottom copyright and legal links.

# Authentication Screens (`/login`, `/register`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`)

Inspired by:

- SyncSpace Official Signature Auth Mockups

Characteristics:

- **Login & Register Split Screen**: 50/50 desktop split layout (`hidden lg:flex w-1/2` for hero panel, `w-full lg:w-1/2` for form). Hides left hero panel on mobile (`< 1024px`) for 100% responsive single-column form experience.
- **Register Hero (Image 1)**: Deep indigo gradient (`from-[#1e1b4b] via-[#0f172a] to-[#020617]`), `NEW ERA OF WORK` pill tag, "Collaborate in real-time, across any distance" headline, interactive multi-cursor document canvas mockup, and "JOIN 10,000+ HIGH-PERFORMING TEAMS WORLDWIDE" footer mark.
- **Login Hero (Image 3)**: Soft pastel gradient, interactive tablet Kanban mockup, "Master your workflow" headline.
- **Form Controls**:
  - 2-Column Social Auth Grid (`Google` + `GitHub`).
  - `─── OR CONTINUE WITH EMAIL ───` section divider line.
  - Inputs: 1px border transitioning to Electric Indigo focus glow (`focus:shadow-[0_0_0_3px_rgba(70,72,212,0.12)]`), 44px (`h-11`) touch target height.
  - Password strength 4-segment indicator bars (`/register`).
  - Primary Action Button: Electric Indigo (`bg-[#4648d4] text-white font-semibold rounded-xl h-11 shadow-xs border-t border-white/20 active:scale-[0.98]`), with right arrow icon (`Sign In →`, `Send Reset Link →`).
- **Forgot & Reset Password (Image 2)**: Centered card layout on tinted canvas (`bg-muted/20`), top Key icon badge, "Reset your password" title, and bottom legal links bar.
